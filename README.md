# The Zest List

A checklist of the twenty writers, poets, painters and composers Ray Bradbury names in his essay "The Joy of Writing" (*Zen in the Art of Writing*, 1990), with three works to explore for each.

Live: https://pouliens.github.io/Zest-List/

Single self-contained `index.html`; checklist progress is saved in the browser via localStorage.

The **Challenge** tab (`#challenge`) tracks Bradbury's daily habit — a short story, a poem, an essay, plus a painting — and a weekly short story of your own, with streaks, a 15-week calendar and JSON backup.

The **Modern SF & fantasy** section (badge: *Pick*) isn't from Bradbury — it's contemporary short fiction (Chiang, Sanderson, Banks, Simmons, Ken Liu, Egan, Le Guin, plus free-to-read quick reads) to feed the nightly short story. The Challenge tab's story suggestions draw from it too.
